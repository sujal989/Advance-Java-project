Return Policy

At EliteWear, we want our customers to be satisfied with every purchase. If you are not completely happy with your order, you may request a return or exchange according to the conditions below.

1. Return Period

Products may be returned within 7 days from the date of delivery. Items returned after this period may not be accepted.

2. Condition of Returned Products

Returned products must be unused, unworn, unwashed and in their original condition. All original tags and packaging should remain attached.

3. Items That Cannot Be Returned

Products that have been damaged, worn, washed or altered by the customer cannot be returned. Certain sale or clearance items may also be non-returnable.

4. Exchanges

If you receive the wrong size or would like to exchange an item, please contact EliteWear. Exchanges will depend on product availability.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged or incorrect product, please contact us as soon as possible. We will review the issue and arrange a replacement or suitable solution.

6. Refunds

Once a returned item has been inspected and approved, the refund will be processed using the appropriate payment method.

7. Delivery Charges

Delivery charges may not be refundable unless the product was damaged, defective or incorrectly supplied by EliteWear.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about a return, exchange or refund, please contact us through our Contact page.
